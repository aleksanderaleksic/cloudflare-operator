3. To create your first tunnel, apply a Tunnel custom resource:

   kubectl apply -f - <<EOF
   apiVersion: networking.cfargotunnel.com/v1alpha1
   kind: Tunnel
   metadata:
     name: example-tunnel
     namespace: {{ include "cloudflare-operator.namespace" . }}
   spec:
     cloudflare:
       email: your-email@example.com
       domain: example.com
       CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN: your-api-token
     size: 1
   EOF

4. Monitor the operator logs:
   kubectl logs -f deployment/{{ include "cloudflare-operator.fullname" . }}-controller-manager -n {{ include "cloudflare-operator.namespace" . }}
